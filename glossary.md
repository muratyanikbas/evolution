# Glossary

## ordered existence

A state of existence in which all things are organized and arranged according to an underlying order, structure, and coherent set of principles.

## darwinian evolution

Evolution as understood and explained according to the principles established by Charles Darwin.

## the modern theory of evolution

?

## the origin of life

How life first emerged from nonliving matter

## to coin a term

To invent or introduce a new word or expression, often to describe a concept that does not yet have an established name.

## biological classification

The systematic organization of living organisms into hierarchical groups based on shared characteristics and "evolutionary relationships".

## taxonomy

The branch of biology concerned with identifying, naming, describing, and classifying living organisms.

## the four mechanisms of evolution

\1. mutation, 2. natural selection, 3. genetic drift, and 4. gene flow.

## heredity of living organisms

The biological process by which genetic information and inherited characteristics are passed from parents to their offspring in living organisms.

## nucleotide

The basic structural unit of DNA and RNA, consisting of three components: a nitrogenous base, a five-carbon sugar (ribose or deoxyribose), and one or more phosphate groups.

## life-forms

Living organisms or types of living organisms, such as animals, plants, fungi, bacteria, and archaea.

## absoluteness of matter

The philosophical view that matter exists independently of consciousness or perception and constitutes the fundamental reality of existence.

## pseudoscientific

Describing claims, beliefs, theories, or practices that are presented as scientific but do not adequately follow scientific methods, standards, or principles.

## well-substantiated

Supported by sufficient, reliable evidence, facts, or sound reasoning.

## biodegradable

Capable of being broken down naturally by microorganisms, such as bacteria and fungi, into simpler substances.

# false statement

A statement that is not true.

## appeared in water by chance

Something came into existence or became visible in water randomly, without deliberate intervention or planning.

## paleontology

The scientific study of life forms that existed during prehistoric or geological periods, particularly through the examination of fossils.

## the study of fossils

The scientific examination and analysis of preserved remains, impressions, or traces of ancient organisms to understand past life on Earth.

## history of life

(In sense of evolution) The study of life on Earth, including its origins, forms, characteristics, and changes throughout Earth's history, from its formation to the present day.

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_life

## fossil record

The total collection of fossils discovered and documented.

## living cell

The smallest structural and functional unit of a living organism, capable of carrying out essential life processes, including metabolism, growth, and reproduction.

## abiogenesis (/ origin of life)

(In the sense of evolution:) The beginning of life on Earth, including the circumstances and processes through which the first living organisms came into existence.

## biopoiesis

The hypothetical process by which living organisms first arose from nonliving matter through natural chemical processes.

## cosmic matter history (chronology of the universe)

How matter emerged, changed, and accumulated across the universe’s lifetime.

## evolution of cells

(In sense of evolution:) The evolutionary origin of cells and their subsequent development through evolutionary processes.

## darwinism

Darwinism is the theory of biological evolution developed by Charles Darwin. The term darwinism is more broadly to refer to Darwin's evolutionary ideas.

## building block of life

A fundamental structural and functional component from which living organisms are made.

## mitosis

The process of cell division in which a eukaryotic cell's nucleus divides, producing two genetically identical nuclei, typically followed by the formation of two daughter cells.

## natural phenomena (natural phenomenon)

*Natural phenomena* are observable events, processes, or occurrences in the natural world that arise from natural causes and can be studied and explained through scientific investigation. The singular of natural phenomena is natural phenomenon.

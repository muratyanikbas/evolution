life-forms: Living organisms or types of living organisms, such as animals, plants, fungi, bacteria, and archaea.

paleontology: The scientific study of life forms that existed during prehistoric or geological periods, particularly through the examination of fossils.

the study of fossils: The scientific examination and analysis of preserved remains, impressions, or traces of ancient organisms to understand past life on Earth.
